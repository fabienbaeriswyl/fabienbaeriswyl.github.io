🇬🇧 Hello and welcome to my personal website!

📰 News:

In November 2025, I received the Prix de Solidarité Confédérale, awarded by the University of Lausanne, for my thesis defended in July 2025.

📚 Bio:

In August 2025, I joined the Institute of Discrete Mathematics and Geometry at TU Wien (Vienna University of Technology) as a postdoctoral researcher, in collaboration with Benedikt Stufler. Between 2020 and 2025, I completed a Double PhD in Mathematics and in Business Analytics, at the Operations Department at Université de Lausanne as well as at the Laboratoire de Probabilités, Statistique et Modélisation at Sorbonne Université, as part of a cotutelle scheme, under the supervision of Valérie Chavez-Demoulin and Olivier Wintenberger. My research was devoted to Poisson cluster point processes, their asymptotic properties, in particular large deviation principles, and their applications.

Prior to this, I undertook a Master in Mathematics and Statistics at McGill University in Montréal, under the supervision of Johanna Nešlehová.
